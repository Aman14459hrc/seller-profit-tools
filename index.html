Hamara kya alag hai

Zyadatar calculator sirf ek profit number dete hain. Hum ye bhi batate hain ki paisa kahan gaya aur aapka product kitna risk jhel sakta hai.

Return-adjusted profitProfit mein returns ka expected nuksaan pehle se joda hota hai, sirf delivered order ka nahi.
Kitne returns tak safeAapka product kitne % returns tak profit mein rahega, ye ek number mein dikhta hai. Saath mein 0% se 50% tak ka stress test.
"Paisa kahan gaya?" barSelling price ka har rupaya dikhta hai: cost, fees, GST, returns, ads aur aapka profit.
Price ladderPrice 5%, 10% ya 20% badhane-ghatane par profit aur margin kitna badlega, ek table mein.
Excel bulk checkPoora catalog upload karo, loss wale products pehle dekho, aur result Excel mein download karo.
